How to read the descriptions (Arabic market terms)

فابريكا = fabrika original factory paint, never repainted — the #1 value signal in Egypt.
رشة = rasha partially repainted (one or more panels).
كسر زيرو = kasr zero a bit over 100,000 km.
ماشية X كم driven X km.
رخصة حتى … registration valid until that date.
معاقين handicap-imported (UAE/import ban lifted), usually low-value tax + documented history.

✅ Before you pay anyone — 4-step check

  1. Licensed examination center — ~few hundred EGP, catches chassis accidents the eye can't.
  2. Registration card (license): original, valid inspection stamp, name matches seller ID. Ask: ١. هل الشاسيه أو الأمام أو الخلف أصلي؟ تم إصلاحه بعد التركيب؟ ٢. الرخصة سارية؟ متى تنتهي؟
  3. Bank meeting: cash deals via Dubizzle → meet at bank/traffic office for same-day transfer (transfer of ownership).
  4. Old cars (E36/Astra/Civic 90s): assume 30–60k reserve budget for post-purchase work even with "factory" ads.
